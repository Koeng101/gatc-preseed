**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `miniprotein_or_nanobody` · Stage: mature · Outreach: moderate · HQ: Monmouth Junction, United States · Founded: 2013

> One-stop synthetic biology service provider offering AI-guided gene synthesis, protein expression, and antibody discovery

## What they do

<cite index="1-1,1-2">Synbio Technologies provides various synthetic biology services, including DNA solutions, RNA solutions, and protein solutions. With their services, customers provide a protein sequence and Synbio handles gene synthesis, expression system selection, protein purification, and functional validation.</cite> <cite index="2-3">Services include hybridoma sequencing, immune repertoire sequencing, antibody design and production, AI-guided heavy chain and light chain pairing, antibody humanization, affinity maturation, and sdAb.</cite>

**AI role**: <cite index="2-2">The company relies on proprietary algorithms to facilitate and expedite the antibody discovery process</cite> and <cite index="3-2">uses AI-guided services in antibody discovery</cite>, with <cite index="10-12">AI-enhanced protein production technologies</cite>.

## Recommended pitch

Position clonal DNA assembly ($50/reaction, 100+ per year volume discounts) as complement to their existing Twist partnership for ultra-short constructs (<2 kb), freeing Synbio's engineering team from routine assembly and allowing them to focus on expression optimization and antibody discovery. Emphasize cost arbitrage: current Twist/IDT/GenScript pricing is 2-3× higher. Offer pilot workflow with 50 short constructs to demonstrate savings and integrate into their antibody discovery pipeline (design → synthesis → expression → AI characterization).

## Why this score

Strong fit on clonal DNA assembly (high demand for short constructs in their customer base), E. coli expression (core competency), and end-to-end binder workflow (AI-guided pairing + production). However, (a) company already has in-house protein production capacity, reducing dependency on external expression services; (b) they already partner with Twist for DNA synthesis, suggesting they may have established vendor relationships; (c) last funding round was 7.5 years ago, indicating potential maturity plateau or shift in growth strategy. Outreach moderate difficulty due to operational maturity and potential procurement inertia. Not tier-1 hot because customer baselineary appears to be established, not early-stage AI teams seeking first vendors.
