# Earendil Labs

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `antibody` · Stage: series_B · Outreach: moderate · HQ: Middletown, United States · Founded: 2021

> AI-native biologics discovery platform generating clinic-ready antibody and bispecific programs at scale.

## What they do

Earendil Labs integrates artificial intelligence across the full biologics R&D lifecycle—from target identification and molecular design to candidate optimization and clinical progression. The company employs generative protein engineering, machine learning, and high-throughput experimental validation to systematically generate differentiated therapeutic programs. Core platform produces antibodies, bispecific antibodies, and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).

**AI role**: AI is positioned as the core 'production engine' for drug discovery and development, not merely a research tool. The platform integrates ML across target identification, sequence generation, property prediction, manufacturability modeling, and experimental design to enable rapid, systematic generation of multiple programs per cycle. Enables both discovery acceleration and multi-parameter optimization of protein therapeutics.
