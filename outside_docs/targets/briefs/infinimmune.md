# Infinimmune

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 1 — Hot) · `antibody` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: Alameda, United States · Founded: 2022

> AI-powered platform that discovers fully human therapeutic antibodies directly from immune-cell libraries via Complete Human™ immunosequencing technology.

## What they do

Infinimmune uses machine learning to systematically surface and engineer rare antibodies from human immune-cell repertoires, advancing them into drug candidates. The company operates an end-to-end discovery platform that pairs complete, natively paired antibodies (both heavy and light chains, all effector regions) from real human immune systems—not synthetic libraries or model organisms.

**AI role**: Machine learning and AI models (including GLIMPSE-1, an antibody-sequence-specific large language model) drive antibody sequence discovery, variant engineering, affinity optimization, and target identification from human B-cell libraries.
