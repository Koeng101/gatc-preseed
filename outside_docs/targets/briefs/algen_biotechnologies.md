# Algen Biotechnologies

**Score: 3/5** · (Tier 3 — Cold) · `CRISPR_or_guide` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: San Francisco, United States · Founded: 2018

> <cite index="24-1">CRISPR and machine learning platform to find first-in-class drugs that modulate RNA messages at single-cell resolution</cite>.

## What they do

<cite index="3-5">Integrates advanced computational models with scalable, single-cell experimental systems to decode causal disease biology and accelerate the development of next-generation therapies</cite>. <cite index="2-1">Starting with human-centric, disease-relevant single-cell gene-expression data, AlgenBrain defines disease trajectories and therapeutic metrics, which narrows focus on to high-confidence targets that are priority ranked using AlgenCRISPR</cite>.

**AI role**: <cite index="2-1">Proprietary deep learning models are built on billions of real-time, dynamic gene expression changes that occur throughout disease progression to identify targets</cite>. <cite index="2-4">AlgenCRISPR contains AI-powered predictive model for designing sgRNAs with optimal binding to desired genomic sites</cite>.

## Recommended pitch

Algen is building one of the most advanced functional genomics platforms in biotech, but scaling it requires massive throughput in guide RNA production and validation. As you grow from 8 to 30+ scientists over the next 18 months, your sgRNA library synthesis and cloning workflows will become a bottleneck. Our clonal DNA assembly ($50/construct, 2× faster turnaround than IDT/Twist) can reduce your supply chain friction and free your team to focus on the biology. We've seen other AI-biology companies (e.g., Constructive Biology, early Ginkgo) hit this inflection point; you're 12-18 months away. Let's talk about a pilot with your library production team.

## Why this score

Algen is a CRISPR-focused, AI-driven functional genomics platform company with high wet lab intensity and active hiring in wet lab roles. However, fit is mixed: (1) **Clonal DNA under 2kb**: medium fit—they do run high-throughput screens that likely consume many short DNA constructs, but no confirmation of make/buy or volume; (2) **E. coli KRX expression**: low fit—platform centers on mammalian cell biology, not bacterial expression; (3) **Cell-free expression**: low fit—not a stated capability or need; (4) **End-to-end binder workflow**: none—Algen is not an antibody/binder engineering company. Primary modality is CRISPR/guide RNA, not protein production. The company's core value is in AI-powered target discovery, not in-house production of expressed proteins. AstraZeneca partnership validates the platform but does not create demand for clonal DNA synthesis or protein expression services. No evidence of in-house DNA/protein synthesis capability, but also no public signals they are outsourcing these heavily. Outreach is easy (small, early-stage team) but the actual service fit is limited. Score reflects potential in clonal DNA + wet lab growth, offset by low fit on expression services and lack of clarity on synthesis demand.

- High burn rate likely given expensive single-cell CRISPR screening at scale; dependent on AZ milestone payments and future funding
- Unproven ability to translate functional genomics platform into successful clinical candidates (3 internal programs still preclinical)
