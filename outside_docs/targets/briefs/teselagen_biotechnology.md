# TeselaGen Biotechnology

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `enzyme` · Stage: series_B · Outreach: moderate · HQ: Portola Valley, USA · Founded: 2011

> AI-powered platform for designing, building, and optimizing biological products through automated DNA design, protocol generation, and DBTL cycle acceleration.

## What they do

TeselaGen develops cloud-based software for synthetic biology and biotech R&D that uses machine learning (Synthetic Evolution® engine) to accelerate the Design-Build-Test-Learn cycle. The platform offers: (1) DNA design and assembly protocol generation supporting Golden Gate, Gibson, MoClo and other modalities; (2) AI agents for combinatorial library design; (3) Electronic Lab Notebook (ELN) with experiment planning and data analysis; (4) Lab automation integration; and (5) Emerging agentic AI Copilot for end-to-end research workflows. Serves pharma, biotech startups, national labs, and academic institutions.

**AI role**: Core platform capability. ML-driven Synthetic Evolution® engine learns from iterative DBTL cycles to predict optimal variants and converge designs 10x faster than random approaches. AI agents now handle library design, protocol generation, data analysis, and experimental prioritization with minimal manual input. Recent alpha of Tesela Copilot applies generative AI to experiment planning and result interpretation.

## Recommended pitch

TeselaGen's Tesela AI is generating hundreds of designed mini-binders, nanobodies, and antibody variants monthly—but your customers must route each design through separate synthesis, expression, and characterization partners. We can collapse that pipeline: our service integrates clonal DNA assembly ($50/construct, <2kb), direct E. coli KRX expression, and Gator BLI binding kinetics in a single workflow. This is the 'express-characterize' layer your platform needs. For your top 10-20 binder-design customers, we can offer white-label integration or direct service, turning Tesela AI output directly into validated binding data—accelerating your DBTL cycle further and deepening customer stickiness. Let's pilot this with a research partner or friendly customer.

## Why this score

TeselaGen is a warm-to-hot prospect for our clonal DNA synthesis and integrated binder workflow services. Scoring rationale: (1) Primary modality (enzyme/protein design) with E. coli expression is core use case, aligning well with our KRX expression and BLI binder workflow service (fit: high/high). (2) Massive DNA synthesis demand signal from iterative DBTL cycles and combinatorial library design (1000-10000 clones/year estimated). (3) Integrated platform positioning means customers expect seamless build-express-characterize workflows; our outsourced services fill exact gap. (4) No in-house wet lab—fully outsource-oriented, no vendor lock-in at protein level. (5) Stage (Series B-range) and headcount (32) suggest moderate procurement friction but agile decision-making. (6) Estabished customer base (pharma, biotech, gov labs) indicates purchasing power and repeatable revenue. Downside: Competitors Benchling and Ginkgo have deeper capital and broader platform strategies; TeselaGen may prioritize large partners (Ansa, NinthBio) for DNA synthesis over new vendors. Recommend warm outreach with focus on (a) 'bring your own DNA' E. coli expression + BLI service for their binder-design teams, and (b) white-label or API integration for end-to-end workflow.
