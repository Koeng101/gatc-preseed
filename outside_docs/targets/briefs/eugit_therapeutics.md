# Eugit Therapeutics

**Score: 3/5** · (Tier 3 — Cold) · `miniprotein_or_nanobody` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: San Carlos, USA · Founded: 2022

> Engineered gamma-delta T cell cancer immunotherapies with TCR-based tissue targeting for precision drug delivery.

## What they do

<cite index="1-4">TAGHOME delivers drugs with precision to diseased tissues using T cell receptors, enhancing safety and improving efficacy.</cite> <cite index="42-7,42-8">The company takes blood from healthy donors and isolates gamma delta T cells, endows them with cancer fighting activity via gene engineering, and produces off-the-shelf cells ready for infusion into cancer patients.</cite> Two distinct programs: (1) gamma delta T cell cancer immunotherapy targeting breast, ovarian, and prostate cancers; (2) TAGHOME platform for tissue-targeted therapeutic delivery, initially focused on Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD).

**AI role**: Limited evidence of AI-biology integration in public materials. Core platform is gene engineering and cell engineering; no indication of AI-driven protein design or computational screening as primary differentiator.
