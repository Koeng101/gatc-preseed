# Nanograb

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 1 — Hot) · `AAV_or_gene_therapy` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: San Francisco, United States · Founded: 2023

> AI-designed multivalent peptide binders on nanoparticle platforms for cell-selective, non-viral gene delivery.

## What they do

Nanograb uses AI and computational physics to design optimized combinations of cell-penetrating peptide binders that attach to nanoparticles for highly targeted, non-viral delivery of gene therapeutics (mRNA, plasmid DNA, CRISPR/Cas9 components, base editors, prime editors) to specific cell types while minimizing off-target binding. The platform exploits multivalency theory to achieve superselective targeting based on cell-surface receptor density and composition.

**AI role**: Core technology: AI-driven optimization of multivalent peptide-binder combinations and their spatial arrangements on nanoparticles, informed by statistical mechanics and computational physics models of receptor-ligand interactions. Uses machine learning to predict which binder combinations will selectively engage targets with desired receptor profiles.
