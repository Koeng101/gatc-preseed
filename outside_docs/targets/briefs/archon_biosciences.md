# Archon Biosciences

**Score: 5/5** · (Tier 1 — Hot) · `antibody` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: Seattle, United States · Founded: 2023

> AI-driven antibody cage (AbC) therapeutics using generative protein design to enhance antibody potency and biodistribution control.

## What they do

Archon directly applies generative protein design to create Antibody Cages (AbCs)—a novel class of biologics combining off-the-shelf antibodies with computationally designed nanostructures that self-assemble into geometrically precise, tunable structures. AbCs integrate antibody binding affinity with engineered control over biodistribution, target engagement, and functional properties, enabling drug development of previously undruggable targets. The company couples its protein design platform with rapid in-house manufacturing and testing.

**AI role**: Generative AI protein design is foundational to the platform. The company uses AI-driven computational tools (leveraging technology from Baker Lab) to generate novel protein structures that form the cage scaffolds. These designs enable exploration of a boundless design space of molecules never seen in nature, optimized for target engagement and biodistribution.

## Risks / concerns

- No disclosed lead program or indication. Preclinical-stage only; earliest clinical trial timeline estimated 2-3+ years away.
- Heavy dependence on David Baker Lab intellectual property and team. Baker is non-voting observer, not executive; continuity risk if key IPD relationships deteriorate.
- Regulatory/manufacturing risk: novel Fc-engineering combined with designed nanostructures may face CMC complexity. Scale-up of AbC assembly validation will be essential before IND.
- Competitive IP landscape: computational protein design tools (RoseTTAFold, diffusion models) increasingly commoditized; defensibility of AbC geometry control vs. alternative nanoscale antibody engineering (e.g., Xencor Xtend, Genmab multispecific, AstraZeneca/Calando nanoparticle scaffolds) unclear.
- Seed-stage capital (17 FTE, 2-year runway per CEO). Series A fundraising will be needed by mid-2026; ability to secure follow-on capital depends on preclinical data and progress selecting lead program.
- Limited published efficacy data. Lazarovits stated 'differentiated efficacy in lower species animal models' but results are unpublished and not presented in detail.
