# 1849 bio

**Score: 3/5** · (Tier 3 — Cold) · `microbial_strain` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: San Francisco, USA · Founded: 2024

> Engineered microbes enabling low-cost, sustainable metal extraction from low-grade and previously uneconomical copper and gold ores.

## What they do

1849 bio designs and engineers microbes for biomining applications. The company develops specialized microorganisms optimized for leaching metals (copper, gold, and others) from low-grade ores and waste materials, particularly targeting difficult-to-process chalcopyrite deposits. Their approach enhances the existing bioleaching process by optimizing the microbial strains themselves rather than just the extraction process.

**AI role**: Limited—not primarily an AI-first company. The firm uses synthetic biology and microbial engineering to design organisms; no explicit mention of AI/ML in platform design, though bioengineering platforms may incorporate computational strain design.

## Why this score

Strong fit on clonal DNA service (high volume of short constructs for strain engineering), but company is not yet engineering binders, antibodies, or proteins at scale. E. coli expression and cell-free have secondary role. Most value is in DNA synthesis bottleneck (iterative design-build-test). Early-stage funding ($500K) means limited budget; however, founders are technically sophisticated and engaged. Minimal fit for full suite; narrow but real demand signal. Outreach is easy (early stage, founder-accessible), but order volume modest relative to mature synbio teams. Not a 4–5 prospect because: (a) no protein expression focus (main service lines 2–3 are less critical), (b) small headcount and budget, (c) microbial strain not miniprotein/nanobody/antibody (our best-fit modalities).
