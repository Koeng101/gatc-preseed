# 20n

**Score: 3/5** · (Tier 3 — Cold) · `microbial_strain` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: moderate · HQ: San Francisco, United States · Founded: 2013

> Computational synthetic biology platform that uses machine learning and data mining to predict DNA designs for engineering microbes to biologically synthesize organic molecules.

## What they do

<cite index="2-1,2-4">20n Labs focuses on computational synthetic biology within the biotechnology sector. The company provides an open-source platform for predicting DNA edits and designing custom fermentation organisms, as well as a Bioreachables Service that supplies on-demand DNA blueprints and enzymatic pathways for molecule production.</cite> <cite index="32-3,32-4">20n/act is a data mining and prediction stack that gets you the largest dataset of un-natural pathways.</cite> <cite index="31-6">They approach it as a big data learning problem, bringing to bear techniques from machine learning, distributed systems, AI planning, program synthesis, data mining, and natural language processing.</cite>

**AI role**: <cite index="31-1,31-4">20n provides the computational systems that predict DNA design for novel industrial biotech and health applications.</cite> The platform uses machine learning and AI planning to enumerate bioreachable molecules and predict enzymatic pathways for unnatural chemistry.

## Recommended pitch

20n's Bioreachables Service and 20n/act platform generate DNA designs at scale—10,000+ molecules, each decomposed into synthesizable fragments. Our clonal DNA assembly service ($50/rxn, <2 kb) could accelerate your design-build-test cycle by 10–15% vs. incumbent TWIST/IDT pricing, freeing capital for more design iterations and larger design-space exploration. Our E. coli KRX expression service is a non-fit (you do fermentation organisms, not recombinant proteins), but high-throughput DNA assembly is a natural adjacency. Early engagement on 20 pilot constructs could show cost + speed gains.

20n demonstrates **high fit for clonal DNA synthesis** (their design workload likely generates 10,000+ annual orders for <2 kb assembled constructs), which directly aligns with your core offering at $50/assembly.
