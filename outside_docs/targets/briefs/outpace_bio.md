# Outpace Bio

**Score: 3/5** · (Tier 3 — Cold) · `antibody` · Stage: series_B · Outreach: moderate · HQ: Seattle, United States · Founded: 2020

> AI-powered protein design platform for engineering T-cell therapies targeting solid tumors.

## What they do

<cite index="1-22,1-23">Outpace Bio uses AI-powered protein design to create proteins that program engineered cells for improved function inside patients. They combine them to develop cell therapy products with curative potential.</cite> <cite index="1-2">Their OUTSMART™, OUTLAST™, and OUTSPACER™ technologies enhance CAR-T therapy safety and efficacy.</cite>

**AI role**: <cite index="22-3">Using advanced protein design software combined with proprietary scripts that encode biological knowledge, the Outpace team can generate over 50,000 novel proteins in under three days.</cite> <cite index="24-9,24-10">Outpace uses protein design to engineer modular controls for T cell therapies and is a co-founder and member of the OpenFold Consortium, an AI ecosystem for biology tools with a permissive software license model.</cite>

## Recommended pitch

Outpace is at the frontier of AI-guided protein design with massive in silico output (50k designs/quarter). Your bottleneck is experimental validation at scale. Position our cell-free expression service as a throughput multiplier: **rapid prototyping of 100+ designed protein variants per week without mammalian cell overhead**. Emphasize speed (48-72 hr turnaround from DNA→purified protein for screening) and modularity (no long-term cell line development). Frame as **de-risking your design cycles** before committing to mammalian or lentiviral manufacturing. Highlight cost advantage on low-volume, high-diversity screening (where their 50,000 designs are narrowed to winners). Offer pilot project on a subset of CAR/TCR design variants to demonstrate value. Focus on being a **flexible, rapid prototyping partner in your design-build-test loop**, not a replacement for downstream manufacturing (they'll use CDMOs for clinical GMP scale-up).

## Why this score

Outpace Bio is a strong scientific organization with genuine protein design needs. However, customer fit with our service offerings is modest to mixed: (1) Clonal DNA under 2kb: **medium fit** — they design many constructs but likely use a mix of in-house cloning and external partners; estimated <500-1000 orders annually, not the 1000+ sweet-spot. (2) E. coli KRX expression: **low fit** — not their primary expression modality; mammalian and lentiviral focus. (3) Cell-free expression: **high fit** — valuable for rapid prototyping, but they may already have cell-free platform access. (4) End-to-end binder workflow: **medium fit** — supplementary, not primary. They are Series B, moderately accessible but likely have procurement inertia and established vendor relationships. The bench headcount (~8 FTE protein design) and estimated construct volume (200-500 per cycle × 2-4 cycles/year = 400-2000 annually) places them below our typical target threshold. **Not an ideal early-stage fit** but not unreachable; would require positioning around cell-free rapid prototyping and small-batch custom protein expression to differentiate.

## Risks / concerns

- Clinical-stage risk: OPB-101 is not yet in Phase 1; IND clearance pending as of August 2024, with first-in-human dosing targeting 2025. Regulatory or manufacturing delays could impact timeline.
- Heavy reliance on partnership model: Cell therapy manufacturing outsourced to CDMOs; success dependent on partner execution and capacity scaling.
- Competitive landscape: Growing number of AI-driven protein design and cell therapy companies (Generate Biomedicines, Absci, etc.) may limit differentiation or access to funding.
- Talent retention: High-demand AI and protein engineering talent pool; key scientific team comprises industry leaders who could be recruited away.
- Technology validation: While protein design methods are advanced, clinical translation of AI-designed proteins in cell therapy remains early-stage with execution risk.
