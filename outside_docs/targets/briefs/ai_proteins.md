# AI Proteins

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 1 — Hot) · `miniprotein_or_nanobody` · Stage: series_B · Outreach: moderate · HQ: Boston, United States · Founded: 2021

> AI-driven de novo miniprotein design platform engineered from scratch using generative AI, synthetic biology, and robotics for therapeutic applications.

## What they do

AI Proteins uses computational de novo protein design combined with AI, synthetic biology, and high-throughput robotics to design entirely novel miniproteins (40–160 amino acids) optimized for specific therapeutic targets. Their platform enables rapid design-build-test cycles, generating therapeutic candidates with complete control over function, stability, and pharmacology without evolutionary constraints.

**AI role**: Generative AI models drive the discovery and optimization engine: AI-enabled design platform rapidly iterates candidate miniproteins, synthetic biology and robotics operationalize production at scale, and machine learning continuously improves models using large experimental datasets from the design-build-test cycle.

## Why this score

Excellent fit: (1) Primary modality miniprotein/nanobody matches bacterial expression systems (E. coli, cell-free). (2) High-throughput design-build-test platform creates strong annual demand for clonal DNA (<2 kb, 1000–10,000 constructs/year). (3) In-house expression and characterization capability exists but still emerging; outsourcing opportunity during scale-up. (4) Early-stage access: post-Series A but pre-public, likely to engage new vendors without entrenched relationships. (5) Technical team credible and founder-led, reducing buyer friction. (6) BMS partnership adds credibility but also procurement risk if BMS vendor relationships dominate. Deduction: Cannot confirm in-house DNA synthesis (no public announcement), so clonal_dna fit remains high. However, lack of mammalian expression need and established in-house protein production capability slightly downgrade expression service fit from 'very high' to 'high.'
