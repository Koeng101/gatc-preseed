# Quantum Sky

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `enzyme` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: moderate · HQ: Cambridge, USA · Founded: 2022

> Quantum-mechanics-informed generative AI platform for protein and RNA design driving next-generation gene editors and therapeutics

## What they do

<cite index="2-1">Quantum Sky enables both protein and RNA functional prediction powered by their SkyEngine platform, which combines generative AI and Quantum Mechanics AI models.</cite> <cite index="31-8,31-9">The company has developed wet-lab validation capabilities that parallel its AI-discovery engine, allowing it to apply the technology to the therapeutics space and build a pipeline of unique gene editors and delivery systems based on expanded amino-acid alphabets.</cite> <cite index="43-3">Quantum Sky operates a hub-and-spoke model, with SkyEngine at its center and each related tool in its own subsidiary, offering potential pharma partners several ways to utilize the company's capabilities.</cite>

**AI role**: <cite index="1-11,1-12">SkyEngine goes beyond the traditional amino acids into all sorts of naturally and artificially modified ones by finding sequences based on how atoms interact within or across proteins and small molecules.</cite> <cite index="31-5">SkyEngine operates on principles of quantum mechanics and requires only modest training sets; its reach encompasses hundreds of non-canonical amino acids and other molecular types.</cite> Core platform; generative design engine for all protein/RNA outputs.
