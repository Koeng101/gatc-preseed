# Engage Bio

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `mRNA_or_RNA` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: San Carlos, USA · Founded: 2021

> Non-viral DNA genetic medicine company using Tethosomes—mRNA-encoded nuclear localizer proteins—to achieve 100x+ expression gain and redosable, durable therapeutics.

## What they do

Engage Bio develops Tethosomes, a proprietary platform delivering therapeutic DNA co-encoded with mRNA that expresses a protein tethering DNA to the nucleus, evading innate immune detection while enabling months-to-years of durable protein production. Applications span gene replacement (cystic fibrosis, monoclonal antibodies), oncology (in situ cancer immunotherapy), and therapeutic protein expression.

**AI role**: No explicit AI/ML platform disclosed; primarily a synthetic biology + molecular engineering company. No evidence of computational protein design or ML-driven optimization in public materials.
