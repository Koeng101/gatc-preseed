# Imperagen

**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `enzyme` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: Manchester, United Kingdom · Founded: 2021

> AI-driven enzyme engineering platform combining machine learning with ultra-fast biophysics simulations and roboticized laboratory automation.

## What they do

Imperagen develops a proprietary Digital Enzyme Evolution™ technology platform for enzyme design and optimization. The platform integrates AI-led protein design, quantitative/molecular mechanics-based simulations, and laboratory automation (robotics, high-throughput screening) to rapidly generate and test enzyme variants. The company targets industrial biocatalysts, enzyme replacements, and drug-manufacturing enzymes across pharmaceuticals, chemicals, and manufacturing.

**AI role**: Core to design and optimization. AI-led protein design combined with biophysics-guided machine learning to predict beneficial mutations and enzyme behavior. Models are trained on experimental feedback from automated variant screening, enabling closed-loop optimization (DBTL cycles).
