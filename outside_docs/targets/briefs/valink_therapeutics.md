> Next-generation bispecific antibody-drug conjugate (bsADC) platform company using modular discovery engine for oncology

## What they do

<cite index="1-1">Valink Therapeutics is a biotechnology company developing first-in-class bispecific antibody-drug conjugates (bsADCs) and other modalities for improved patient outcomes.</cite> <cite index="6-6">The company combines protein engineering, synthetic biology, automation, and AI</cite> via its <cite index="6-7">modular high-throughput screening platform, LiliumX™, which enables the company to break down each ADC into its building blocks</cite> for rapid iteration.

**AI role**: <cite index="25-13">Valink is powered by the LiliumX® platform, a proprietary discovery engine built on three pillars: synthetic protein biology, high-throughput functional screening, and AI.</cite>

## Recommended pitch

Valink's LiliumX platform generates thousands of bispecific antibody designs per cycle but is rate-limited on functional characterization (BLI) during iterative screening phases. Our end-to-end binder workflow—combining rapid DNA synthesis, mammalian expression, and Gator BLI quantification—can parallelize your design iteration cycles and compress time-to-lead-optimization. We scale from 10 to 10,000 candidates per round without the capital cost of expanded in-house BLI infrastructure. Talk to us about offloading 1–3 rounds of intermediate screening while your team focuses on lead development and IND-enabling studies.

## Why this score

Strong technical fit on end-to-end binder workflow (potentially 1000–10000 annual orders if BLI becomes rate-limiting), but weak fit on core clonal DNA and E. coli services. Valink's primary limiting factor is mammalian expression throughput + BLI capacity for iterative design, not short-construct synthesis or bacterial expression. They likely already outsource DNA synthesis via established low-cost vendors (Twist, IDT) or have internal synthesis. Our E. coli KRX service is wrong host for their IgG + ADC focus. Clonal DNA under 2kb is useful but not differentiated vs. competitors. Outreach is easy (seed stage, founder-accessible, no vendor lock), but commercial upside is limited to niche BLI bottleneck. Recommend positioning as BLI throughput partner rather than synthesis vendor if engaging.
