> AI-powered drug discovery platform mining parasite and viral proteins for autoimmune disease therapeutics

## What they do

<cite index="1-23,1-27">Ditto transforms parasite strategies for hacking the immune system into breakthrough drugs for autoimmune disease, mining the genomes of viruses, ticks, and worms</cite>. <cite index="1-4,1-5">Since founding seven months ago, they have analyzed 1M+ proteins across primate-infecting viruses, ticks, and parasitic worms and discovered thousands of proteins predicted to hit clinically validated human targets</cite>.

**AI role**: <cite index="1-41">They use AI to identify proteins and engineer them into next-generation autoimmune therapies</cite>. AI is central to their platform: computational screening of >1M parasite/viral proteins to identify drug-like candidates with predicted human target engagement.

### Service fit

| Service | Fit | Est. annual orders | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clonal DNA (<2 kb) | high | 1000-10000 | Platform mines 1M+ proteins and has identified thousands of candidates for validation. Design-build-test-learn cycle on parasite protein variants and human target scaffolds will require iterative synthesis of gene constructs <2 kb (ORFs for expression tags, domain variants, binding-domain variants). At discovery phase with thousands of hits, high throughput DNA synthesis is essential. |
| E. coli KRX expression | high | 1000-10000 | Parasite proteins and engineered scaffolds are typically soluble and bacterial-expressible. Early validation of binding and function requires rapid, low-cost expression for kinetic assays (BLI). Thousands of candidate proteins require expression for characterization. E. coli KRX with immediate purification aligns perfectly with their rapid-iteration discovery workflow. |
| Cell-free expression | medium | 100-1000 | Cell-free may be useful for toxic protein variants or rapid prototyping during lead optimization, but unlikely to be primary need. Bacterial expression should suffice for most parasite-derived proteins. |
| End-to-end binder workflow | high | 100-1000 | Ditto is explicitly mining protein candidates for binding and immune modulation. They mention 1–2 nM binding affinity as a design goal. An end-to-end workflow (DNA → expression → BLI characterization) would dramatically accelerate their candidate validation pipeline. They are at stage where building in-house BLI throughput may be capital-intensive; outsourced binder characterization service fits their stage perfectly. |
