**Score: 4/5** · (Tier 2 — Warm) · `enzyme` · Stage: seed_to_series_A · Outreach: easy · HQ: New York, USA · Founded: 2024

> Bioengineered precision biocides targeting pathogenic agricultural pests via AI-discovered biological pathways

## What they do

ACX develops next-generation biopesticides using synthetic biology and AI to identify and target conserved biological pathways across pathogenic species affecting crops and animals. Their platform combines bioengineering with precision drug-delivery systems (nanoparticle-based) to minimize toxicity while achieving high specificity and efficacy against target pests.

**AI role**: AI used to identify common biological pathways across pathogenic species; CTO Melina Petsolari holds PhD in Computer Science with background in NLP and HCAI
